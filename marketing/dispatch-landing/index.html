Getting it live

Built for your shop, not configured from a template.

You don’t write prompts, pick a model, or learn a dashboard. We build it, you approve it. Most installs are answering live calls in about two weeks, depending on how fast we can get into your phone system and scheduler.

01

ListenDays 1–3

We go through your recorded calls, your price book, your service area and your booking rules — including the jobs you don’t want. The agent learns how your best person actually talks, not how a script says to.

02

BuildDays 4–8

We write the agent, wire it into your phone system and your scheduler, and set the handoff rules: what it books itself, what it flags, what goes straight to a human.

03

You try to break itDays 9–12

You call it. Your CSRs call it. You throw it the weird ones — the commercial job, the angry callback, the caller who won’t give an address. We fix what it gets wrong and you call again.

04

Go live, narrowAround day 14

It starts on overflow only — the calls that are ringing out today. Once you’ve read a week of transcripts and you like what you see, you widen it.

05

Tune, monthlyOngoing

We read the transcripts with you, find where it fumbled or where a real person should have taken over sooner, and adjust. Your price book and your hours change; so does the agent.

Straight answers

What Dispatch does not do.

Worth reading before you book the call, so neither of us wastes the half hour.

It doesn’t quote repairs.

It can state your diagnostic or trip fee because that’s a fixed number. Anything that needs eyes on the equipment gets quoted on site, by your tech.

It doesn’t pretend to be a person.

Ask it directly and it says it’s an AI assistant, then keeps booking. We build it that way on purpose — callers who find out later feel tricked, and that costs you the job.

It doesn’t replace your CSR.

Your people keep the calls they’re already answering well. Dispatch takes what’s currently rolling to voicemail, and hands anything delicate straight back to a human.

It won’t fix a broken funnel.

If the leads are bad or the techs can’t get there for nine days, answering faster only surfaces that sooner. We’ll tell you on the call if that’s what we think is happening.

Questions we get

Before you book.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many calls you take, which of the three channels you turn on, and what we have to integrate with. There’s a build-and-training fee up front and a monthly for running and tuning it. We quote it on the call once we know your volume — we’d rather give you a real number than a page price you’ll have to renegotiate.

Will it work with my phone system?

If your numbers live with a cloud provider — RingCentral, Dialpad, Vonage, Twilio, 8×8, Zoom Phone and the like — yes, and forwarding is usually a same-day change. If you’re on an old on-prem PBX, tell us the make on the call; sometimes the answer is a small hardware change first, and sometimes it’s “not yet.”

Does it book into ServiceTitan / Jobber / Housecall Pro?

Those are the common ones and they’re the ones we wire up most often. If you’re on something unusual, it may book into a shared calendar and push the job details to your team instead of writing directly into the system. We’ll confirm which it is before you sign anything.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

It will, occasionally — so the design assumes it. Every call is transcribed and you can read any of them. Emergencies and anything outside its rules get handed to a person rather than guessed at. When something does go sideways, we find it in the monthly transcript review and change the rule that allowed it.

Can I hear it on my own business before I commit?

Yes. The demo above is a fictional HVAC company so you can beat on it without touching anything real. If you want to hear it as your shop, we can put a rough version together using your price book and service area before you commit to the full build.

How long until it’s answering live calls?

Around two weeks for a typical single-trade shop, assuming we get phone system access and your booking rules in the first few days. Multi-location or heavy integration work takes longer, and we’ll say so up front rather than after you’ve signed.
