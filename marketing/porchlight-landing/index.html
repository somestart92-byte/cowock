Getting it live

Nothing goes live until you say it’s ready.

You don’t write prompts, pick a model, or learn a dashboard — we build it, you approve it. We don’t put a day count on this. Most shops are live in three to five weeks, and the variable isn’t us: it’s how fast we get into your phone system, and how long step three takes. Step three ends when you’re satisfied, not when the calendar says so.

Straight answers

What Porchlight does not do.

Worth reading before you book the call, so neither of us wastes the half hour.

It doesn’t quote repairs.

It states your diagnostic or service-call fee per trade, because those are fixed numbers, and it says when an estimate is free. Anything needing eyes on the job gets quoted on site, by your tech.

It doesn’t pretend to be a person.

Ask it directly and it says it’s an AI assistant, then keeps booking. We build it that way on purpose — callers who find out later feel tricked, and that costs you the job.

It doesn’t replace your CSR.

Your people keep the calls they already answer well. Porchlight takes what’s currently rolling to voicemail, and hands anything delicate straight back to a human.

It doesn’t talk about insurance claims.

On storm damage it books the inspection and notes that the tech documents everything for the adjuster. It won’t tell a homeowner what their policy covers or what their deductible is — it has no way to know.

It doesn’t coach repairs over the phone.

Shutting off water, gas or a breaker for safety is the only thing it ever asks a caller to do with their own hands. It won’t walk anyone through opening a panel.

It won’t fix a broken funnel.

If the leads are bad or the techs can’t get there for nine days, answering faster only surfaces that sooner. We’ll tell you on the call if that’s what we think is happening.

Questions we get

Before you book.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many calls you take, how many of the three gaps you want covered, and what we have to integrate with. There’s a build-and-training fee up front and a monthly for running and tuning it. We quote it on the call once we know your volume — we’d rather give you a real number than a page price you’ll have to renegotiate.

Does it really handle all four trades, or just the one?

All four. It works out the trade from the first sentence, asks the triage questions that trade actually needs, and quotes that trade’s fee — the plumbing service call is not the electrical diagnostic. It also knows what your shop doesn’t do: appliance repair, pools, septic, commercial buildings. Those get a polite referral instead of a booking. Test it on the demo; that’s what it’s there for.

Will it work with my phone system?

If your numbers live with a cloud provider — RingCentral, Dialpad, Vonage, Twilio, 8×8, Zoom Phone and the like — yes, and forwarding is usually a same-day change. If you’re on an old on-prem PBX, tell us the make on the call; sometimes the answer is a small hardware change first, and sometimes it’s “not yet.”

Does it book into ServiceTitan / Jobber / Housecall Pro?

Those are the common ones and the ones we wire up most often. If you’re on something unusual, it may book into a shared calendar and push the job details to your team instead of writing directly into the system. We’ll confirm which it is before you sign anything.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

It will, occasionally — so the design assumes it. Every call is transcribed and you can read any of them. Emergencies and anything outside its rules get handed to a person rather than guessed at. When something does go sideways, we find it in the monthly transcript review and change the rule that allowed it.

Can I hear it as my own business before I commit?

Yes. The demo above is Ridgeway Home Services, a fictional four-trade shop, so you can beat on it without touching anything real. If you want to hear it as your company, we can put a rough version together using your price book and service area before you commit to the full build.
